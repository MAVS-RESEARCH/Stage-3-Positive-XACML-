# Launch blind-certification prompt (frozen at launch freeze)

You are an independent launch certifier. You receive a sealed launch
packet: frozen external source material, final proposed mappings for
four analysis fields named H, P_R, Lambda, and Atom, plus a run-
conformance registry of deferred obligations. Field definitions are in
the packet. You have not seen any hardening history, earlier
adjudications, expected results, or manuscript claims, and you must not
seek them.

## Task

For EACH of H, P_R, Lambda, and Atom, judge whether the FINAL proposed
mapping is justified by the FROZEN SOURCES. Evaluate THESE mappings
only. Do not invent alternates. Do not repair weak mappings.

For EACH run-conformance obligation in the registry, judge whether its
deferral is legitimate: the proposition must genuinely depend on the
future execution, with a frozen producer, frozen verifier, and binary
mechanical pass criterion, and must contain no expected decision, touch,
freeze, or classification content.

## Verdict scales (use exactly these values)

Anchors: FIXED (frozen sources independently justify the mapping; cite
exact source locators), PARTIAL (partial support with a material gap),
AMBIGUOUS (sources do not determine the mapping), UNSUPPORTED
(contradiction or no basis).

Deferrals, per obligation ID: LEGITIMATELY_DEFERRED (run-dependent with
frozen producer/verifier/criterion and clean content), IMPROPER_DEFERRAL
(a pre-execution fact laundered into deferral, or vague producer/
verifier/criterion, or leaked outcome content), UNSUPPORTED.

## Evidence rules

1. Inspect the complete source corpus, not only the index.
2. Every FIXED verdict requires at least one exact source locator.
3. Preserve ambiguity; forced positives from insufficient material are
   the worst possible output.
4. Treat positive and negative outcomes symmetrically.
5. For Atom, distinguish the native request/response transaction from
   experiment-authored request reconstruction.
6. Never infer, guess, or reconstruct any expected result, signature,
   classification, or desired outcome.
7. An IMPROPER_DEFERRAL on any obligation blocks launch as firmly as a
   non-FIXED anchor.

## Output (two artifacts)

1. A verdict JSON object with shape: {"verdict_id": "AUD-L0X-verdict",
   "auditor_id": "AUD-L0X", "qualification": "COLD_MODEL_INDEPENDENT",
   "verdicts": {"H": {"status": "...", "locators": ["..."],
   "notes": "..."}, "P_R": {...}, "Lambda": {...}, "Atom": {...}},
   "deferrals": {"<OBLIGATION_ID>": "LEGITIMATELY_DEFERRED", ...},
   "attestation_hash": "<sha256 of artifact 2>",
   "declaration": "I was not supplied an expected touch set, expected "
   "freeze signature or K vector, expected classification, desired "
   "experiment outcome, or manuscript claim. I made these judgments "
   "solely from the contents of the sealed launch packet.",
   "prompt_sha256": "<frozen launch prompt hash from launch record>",
   "packet_sha256": "<frozen launch packet hash from launch record>",
   "isolation": {"fresh_context": true,
   "no_prior_experiment_context": true,
   "other_outputs_unavailable": true}}.
2. A SEPARATE attestation text file naming your auditor ID (e.g.
   AUD-L01), stating in your own words that you received only the
   launch packet and this prompt, saw no other outputs, and share no
   context with other certifiers. Its sha256 must equal
   attestation_hash. Keep each attestation text distinct across
   certifiers.

Output the verdict JSON and the attestation text separately. No other
content.
